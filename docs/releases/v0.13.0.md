# Obsi Bible 0.13.0

- **Looks and works like the other Obsi apps.** Same colours, fonts, tab bar, menus and settings as ObsiRead.
- **The Bible opens on its own page.** No tab bar while you read. Back takes you home.
- **Save verses with the shared save sheet.** Pick a template, vault and folder, like in ObsiRead.
- **Reading settings are shared.** Text size, font and line spacing match your other Obsi apps.
- **New ⋮ menu in the reader.** Pick which buttons show at the top.
- Your settings, vault and last chapter move over by themselves.
