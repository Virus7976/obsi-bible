# Obsi Bible 0.7.0

Obsi Bible, ObsiTube and ObsiRead now work as one set.

- Home has "Your other apps": open ObsiTube or ObsiRead in one tap, and see your newest highlights from them.
- Search also finds words in your video and article highlights.
- Notes shows your ObsiTube and ObsiRead highlights too, under "All apps". Tap one to open it in its own app, right at that spot.
- Share a Bible link (bible.com, Bible Gateway, esv.org, Bible Hub, Blue Letter Bible) or a verse like "John 3:16" to Obsi Bible, and it opens there.
- Share a video or web page to Obsi Bible by mistake, and it goes to ObsiTube or ObsiRead.
- A verse in ObsiTube or ObsiRead opens here with one tap.
- Settings has "Your Obsi apps".
- Only the Obsi apps can read your highlights. Sharing them needs Android 12 or later.
