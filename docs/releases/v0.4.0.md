## Obsi Bible 0.4.0
- Read the ESV. Paste your free key from api.esv.org in Versions.
- Listen to Crossway's own ESV recording. It streams with your key.
- Add Bibles from API.Bible with your free key. The Message shows if your key is allowed to use it.
- Up to 500 verses of an online Bible stay on the phone, so recent chapters work offline.
- Online Bibles show their copyright under the page.
- When you send to Obsidian, pick a template from your vault's Templates folder.
- Add tags and links to a note before you send it.
- Obsidian's {{date:FORMAT}} and {{title}} work in your templates.
