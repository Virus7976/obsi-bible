## Obsi Bible 0.2.0
- The whole Berean Standard Bible is in the app. It works with no internet.
- The reader looks like the reference: big chapter number, grey verse numbers, italic headings, and footnotes you can tap.
- Listen to any chapter. Pick a recorded narrator (Bob Souer, Barry Hays or Jordan Gilbert), the phone's own voice, or a Microsoft voice with your own key.
- The page follows along. The verse being read is marked, and with the phone voice each word lights up as it is spoken.
- Speed, sleep timer, and controls on the lock screen. Audio goes on to the next chapter by itself.
- Search finds books, references like "eph 3:20", and words in every verse.
- Tap a verse to play from there, copy it or share it.
- The app checks for updates in the background and sends a notification when a new version is ready.
