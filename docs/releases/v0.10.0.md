# Obsi Bible 0.10.0

## Across every Obsi app

- **One review.** Review now shows the due highlights of all your Obsi apps in one deck. Keep, Drop and Not now go back to the app the highlight is from. The Review count on Home counts them all.
- **One search.** Search shows results from your other Obsi apps too, under "In your other apps". Tap one to open it in its app.
- **One backup.** Settings › Backup saves every Obsi app to one file. "Put one back" restores all of them, for example on a new phone. Downloads are not in the file.
- **New stats page.** Pick a week, a month, a year or all time, and the apps to count. It shows how the time compares with before, a chart, the streak with a calendar, the hours you learn most, what you spent most time on, and your highlights by colour.

## Obsi Bible

- Time reading the Bible counts toward the shared stats now. Home has the stats card.
