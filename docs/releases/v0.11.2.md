# Obsi Bible 0.11.2

- **Tag ideas use your tags.** Your own tags come first: every one that fits is suggested. A new tag is added only for a topic none of yours covers. New tags are one word. When an idea is close to a tag you have, like "habit" and your "habits", you get yours.
- **Better tag ideas.** Tags now name topics to find a quote by later, like a theme, a field or a person. They no longer copy words from your note, and filler tags like "note" are gone. You get up to 6 ideas, and as many new tags as fit, not only 2.
