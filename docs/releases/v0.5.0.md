## Obsi Bible 0.5.0
- A new setup guide walks you through every key, with a link for each step. It opens on first start.
- Open the guide again any time from Settings or Versions.
- Each key has a Check button that tries it and says what it found.
- The Message: add it through API.Bible or YouVersion, if your key is allowed to use it.
- New: Bibles from YouVersion with your own free app key.
- Settings has a search box.
- API.Bible now uses its new address, and its Bibles show a link to api.bible.
- Saved online text is read again after 30 days, as API.Bible asks.
- Online Bibles play only their own recordings. A computer voice is not allowed for them.
