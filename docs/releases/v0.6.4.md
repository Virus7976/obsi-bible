# Obsi Bible 0.6.4

- Highlights show in The Message too. The Message prints verses in groups, such as 1-4. A highlight on any verse in the group now covers the whole group.
- Tap a verse group to pick all of it.
- Search filters: type a word or a part of the Bible, then tap Keep or Only in. Add more words to narrow the search. Backspace in an empty box takes off the last filter.
- Parts of the Bible you can pick: Old and New Testament, Law, History, Poetry and wisdom, Prophets, Gospels, Paul's letters, Letters, and any book.
- Your highlights in search only show when they have the words you typed.
