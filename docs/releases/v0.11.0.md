# Obsi Bible 0.11.0

- **Find by meaning.** In search, one quiet row finds your highlights and passages about an idea, even when they use other words. Search "rest" and it also finds what you saved about sleep. You always see your own words, as you saved them, never text written for you. Each result opens where it is, in its own app. Nothing is sent until you tap the row. This needs an AI service or a model on the phone (see below).
- **Tag ideas.** When you add a quote, a few tags that fit appear once you finish your note; tap one to add it. They read the quote and your note together. Nothing is sent while you type. Tags you already use come first, so the same idea always gets the same tag, and search finds it later with no AI at all. In Settings › AI service › Tag ideas, pick "By themselves" or "When I tap".
- **Your week goes into your weekly note in Obsidian.** Settings › Weekly note. It finds your setup by itself: Periodic Notes, Calendar, Journals, Templater, or notes you make by hand. A new note is made from your own template. The apps write time spent, what you finished, and your highlights from every Obsi app between two hidden markers, under the heading you pick. The rest of the note stays yours. It updates when you leave the app. No AI.
- **A home-screen widget.** "Highlight of the day" shows one of your highlights from any Obsi app, your streak, and how many highlights wait for review. Tap the highlight to open it where it is. No AI.
- **AI service setup, once for every app.** Settings › AI service. Pick OpenRouter, Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Groq, Mistral, DeepSeek, xAI, Ollama or any other service, paste your own key, and pick a model from its list. It is off until you set it up. The apps send only short pieces of text, as little as the job needs, and keep every result on the phone so nothing is sent twice. Everything else works without it. Your key stays on the phone.
- **AI on the phone, with no internet.** Settings › AI service › On this phone. The apps look at your phone first. When it has Gemini Nano built in, they use it. If not, and the phone has the memory, they suggest a small free model to download on Wi-Fi (1.6 GB). One download serves every Obsi app. You can also use a model file you already have.
- **When the AI cannot work, it steps aside.** The apps use your service first. With no internet, the model on the phone takes over. When nothing works, find by meaning and tag ideas are simply not shown, and Settings › AI service says why.
- **What's new.** Settings › What's new lists the changes in every version. After an update, a short card shows what changed.
- **Bible highlights are flashcards in the other apps.** In review in ObsiTube, ObsiRead or ObsiLearn, one key word of a verse is hidden. Think of it, then tap Show.
- **Ollama and services on your own computer now work.** Plain http:// addresses were blocked before.
