# Obsi Bible 0.13.1

- **One Obsi folder in your vault.** Bible notes now go in Obsi/Bible, and highlights files in Obsi/Bible/Highlights. All Obsi apps share the Obsi folder.
- Your old Bible notes and highlights files move there by themselves, one time. Your own notes in a "Bible" folder stay where they are.
- A folder you picked yourself does not change.
