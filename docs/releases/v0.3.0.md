## Obsi Bible 0.3.0
- Highlight verses in six colours. Tap a verse, then tap a colour.
- Drag the two handles to highlight only the words you want.
- Write a note on any verse, with tags. A note icon shows in the margin beside it.
- In a note, link to a note in your vault or to another verse. Tap Link, or type [[.
- Send a highlight to Obsidian as a new note, or add it to one running note. Each note links back to its verse.
- Pick your vault folder once, and notes are saved straight into it.
- The You tab shows everything you highlighted and wrote, newest first.
- The new Notes tab lists your notes by book or by tag. It takes the place of Plans.
- Search puts the best match first, fixes typos, and also finds your own highlights, notes and #tags.
- After you allow installs from the app, the update goes on by itself.
