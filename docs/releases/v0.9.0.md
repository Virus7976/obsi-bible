# Obsi Bible 0.9.0

- **Light, dark or black, shared.** Pick it here or in another Obsi app, and the others follow. Bible keeps its own colours.
- **Setup uses your vault.** When another Obsi app has your vault, the walk-through links it in one tap.
- If Android blocks the sharing (an app was installed in the wrong order), setup says which app to install again.
- New app icons with no white edge, in five colours. Settings › Look.
- Clearer errors when a Bible or a voice cannot be reached.
