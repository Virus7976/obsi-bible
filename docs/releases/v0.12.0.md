# Obsi Bible 0.12.0

- **Obsidian can use the AI model on this phone.** In Obsidian, the Obsi Clips plugin can link to this app's model to find clips close in meaning and suggest tags. The app asks you first, and nothing leaves the phone.
- **See and remove linked apps.** Settings, AI, Other apps on this phone lists each app that may use the model, with Remove. A small notification shows while it is on. It stops by itself after 20 minutes unused.
