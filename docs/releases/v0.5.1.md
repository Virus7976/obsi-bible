# Obsi Bible 0.5.1

- The Message is added by itself when your API.Bible or YouVersion key has it. Under each key, the app says what the key has.
- Poems wrap better. Indents are narrower, and a wrapped line sits deeper than the next line.
- The copyright line moved to the end of the chapter. It no longer sits above the play bar.
- Scroll away while the audio plays, and a "Back to verse" button appears.
- The audio pauses while you write a note or send to Obsidian. It goes on when you close it. Turn this off in Settings, Listening.
- Close a note without writing anything, and your picked verses stay picked.
- Search no longer leaves a big empty space above the keyboard.
